▶ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Is Tabox a session manager or a tab manager?
A: Both. Tabox saves complete browsing sessions (all windows and tab groups) and also lets you curate collections tab by tab, so it replaces both a session manager and a "read later" pile of bookmarks.

Q: How is Tabox different from bookmarks?
A: Bookmarks save one page at a time and quickly turn into a junk drawer. Tabox saves entire sessions with their tab-group structure, restores them in one click, keeps them synced across devices, and lets you share them as living, collaborative collections.

Q: What happens to my tab groups?
A: They're preserved exactly, including names, colors, and tab order. When you restore a collection, your tab groups come back just as you saved them.

Q: Does Tabox slow down my browser?
A: No, the opposite. Saving a collection lets you close dozens of tabs, freeing memory and CPU while keeping everything one click away.

Q: Do I need an account?
A: No account is needed for saving and organizing locally. Sign in with Google only if you want cross-device sync, sharing, or AI features.

Q: Does Tabox work on Microsoft Edge or Brave?
A: Yes. Tabox supports Chrome, Edge, Brave, and other Chromium-based browsers, including AI features.

Q: Can I recover tabs if I close a window by accident?
A: If the window was saved (or auto-updating) in a collection, yes. Just restore it. Many users keep an auto-updated collection as a live safety net.
